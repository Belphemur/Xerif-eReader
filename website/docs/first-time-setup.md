# First-time setup

Xerif boots straight into the Home screen — nothing to sign up for.

## On first launch

- The Home screen points at your last-read book (or an empty library on a
  fresh SD card).
- **Home** shows: Reading (most recent book), Browse Files, Library,
  File Transfer, and Settings.
- On later restarts the firmware reopens the book you were reading and
  the page you left.

## Wi-Fi and books

You do not need Wi-Fi to read. Set it up only when you want wireless
book transfer or progress sync:

- **File Transfer → Join a Network** connects to your home Wi-Fi, or
  **Create Hotspot** makes the reader its own network.
- Books load over USB (SD card in the reader mounts as a USB drive in
  File Transfer mode) or over Wi-Fi from the [web interface](/docs/web-interface).

See [Loading books](/docs/loading-books) for the details.
