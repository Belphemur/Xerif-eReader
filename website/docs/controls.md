# Controls

Buttons and touch behavior are configurable under **Settings › Controls**.

## Buttons

| Location | Buttons |
| --- | --- |
| Bottom edge | Back, Confirm, Left, Right |
| Right side | Power, Side Up, Side Down, Reset |

- **Power:** hold briefly to sleep/wake; reset with **Reset** + holding
  Power. Double-click can be assigned — on X4 Pro it toggles the
  frontlight.
- **Front buttons:** remappable, with orientation-aware layouts and a
  shared long-press action.
- **Side buttons:** page-turn layouts (either side, or buttons-only
  mode), plus a long-press page action.

## Touch

- **Swipe down from the top edge** opens the Control Center from almost
  any screen: night mode, screen refresh, reading direction, reader touch
  toggle, frontlight brightness and warmth (drag the sliders), and
  Bluetooth page-turner on builds that have it.
- **Page-turn gestures** while reading are configurable (tap zones and
  swipes).
- Touch can be turned off entirely while reading to avoid accidental
  turns.

## Screenshots

Press **Power + Side Down** together, or open the reader menu
(**Confirm** while reading) and pick **Take screenshot**. Screenshots
land in the `screenshots/` folder on the SD card.

## Long press

The page-turn long-press action is shared between front and side button
groups — change it in either place and it applies to both.
