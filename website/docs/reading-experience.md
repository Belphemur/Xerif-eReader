# Reading experience

Xerif's reader is built around a real typographic engine: fractional glyph
metrics, kerning, ligatures, and anti-aliased grayscale text laid out once
and cached, so page turns stay fast.

## Typography

Under **Settings › Reader** you control the reading experience:

- **Font family and size** — built-in fonts, or any `.ttf`/`.otf`/`.ttc`
  you put on the SD card (see [Custom fonts](/docs/custom-fonts)).
- **Line spacing, margins, paragraph alignment and spacing.**
- **Hyphenation** on or off.
- **Text anti-aliasing** for smooth grey letter edges (slightly slower
  page turns).

## While reading

- Tap or press to turn pages; long-press for chapter navigation.
- Press **Confirm** to open the reader menu: chapter list, bookmarks,
  clippings and highlights, screenshots.
- **Dictionary lookup:** select a StarDict dictionary under
  **Settings › Reader › Dictionary**, then look up a word or phrase while
  reading — see [Dictionary lookup](/docs/dictionary).
- **Focus Reading** (optional) bolds the first part of each word to guide
  your eye — see [Focus Reading](/docs/focus-reading).
- **Reading statistics** — per-book stats, reading speed, streaks, and
  rhythm screens; see [Reading statistics](/docs/reading-statistics).
- Auto page turn can advance the page at a set interval.

## Links and footnotes

EPUB internal links, footnotes, and endnotes are tappable where the book
provides them.
